# 90-second demo video — script and shot list

Record the phone frame at https://kyc-preflight.vercel.app (Chrome, screen recording) with your voice over. Run it once before recording so Live AI is warm. Use the "Jewellery on Instagram" preset, choose "On my website or app" with a store URL, and the "short" bank sample so the fix moment happens.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | Landing page, scroll to "The problem vs the fix" | "New sellers on Razorpay pay ₹199 for KYC, and often find out afterwards that their category, bank name or website won't pass. On Trustpilot, Play Store and Reddit, 'paid, then rejected, no reason' is the most repeated complaint." |
| 8–15 s | Phone: welcome screen with the empty pass | "KYC Pre-flight runs those checks first, inside the merchant app, before the pay button. Four stamps, ninety seconds." |
| 15–30 s | Tap Start → mic icon → say or pick the jewellery description → result card, open "Also considered" | "Priya describes her shop in Hinglish. We match it against Razorpay's own 340 sub-categories: a retriever picks eight, the model chooses only among those, and a fixed policy decides. If the answer depends on something she hasn't said, it asks one question instead of guessing." |
| 30–42 s | "Where will customers pay you?" → website → check runs → one red row → "Write this page for me" → draft appears | "She'll sell on her site, so we open it the way a reviewer does and check the five policy pages reviewers reject you for. The refund policy is missing, so it writes one for her business. She reviews it and copies it." |
| 40–50 s | KYC route: consent → video call → prep list → slot | "She isn't in the KYC registry, so she'll need a video call. Today that's a surprise after paying. Here she sees what to keep ready and books the slot now." |
| 50–65 s | Names: read documents → bank shows red "Partial match" → tap "Draft the letter for my branch" (flash it) → "Use my other account" → green | "The model reads her PAN, Aadhaar and bank proof. A deterministic engine scores the match the way the ₹1 bank check will. LAKSHMI is missing on the bank account, so it would stall. She switches accounts; it passes." |
| 65–75 s | Go-live pass, four green stamps, pay button enabled → submitted timeline | "Only when nothing is red does the pay button appear. The fee is charged after the checks, not before." |
| 75–88 s | "See how it works" → scorecard numbers | "It's measured: 146 labelled descriptions, 91% told the right status, zero blocked businesses told 'supported', 14 of 14 adversarial re-wordings caught. 304 name pairs, zero false passes. Anyone can run the eval live." |
| 88–90 s | Landing: "Why it isn't built yet" | "Every reason she'd be rejected, found and fixed before she pays. The model reads and writes; the rules decide." |

Tips: speak slightly faster than feels natural; the cuts carry it. If a live call is slow, pause the recording and resume after it returns. Keep the cursor still while you talk.
